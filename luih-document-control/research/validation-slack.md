## B. 12-channel sample

Window: 2026-04-11 to 2026-10-11 (6 months).

Method:
- **Top-level**: channel posts excluding joins, leaves and deleted messages.
- **Replies**: the thread reply counts Slack reports. These include the LUIH OS bot's own threaded change detail.
- **Doc posts**: top-level posts announcing a document or set saved, updated, submitted or approved. These cover plans, Design, Cabinet and Fabuwood books, L-1, D-1, D-2, surveys, permit and approved-plans posts, and specbook bot posts.
- **V** (version questions): a message asking which document or version is current, where a document is, or what changed in a new version; or saying a document or spec is missing, not visible in BT, or contradicts another document.
- **I** (incidents): see section C.
- **Coverage**: every top-level post was read. Threads were opened when the scan or keyword search flagged them (about 30 threads). Keyword searches across all 12 channels used "version", "don't see", "match" and "old". V and I are therefore lower bounds.
